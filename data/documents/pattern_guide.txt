Q: What are patterns?, What are pattern IDs?, What is a pattern Template? What is a Pattern Based Skin?

A: Some skins in Counter Strike are known as “Pattern Based.” This means the texture for the skin is actually a piece of artwork laid onto the weapon in a specific orientation. This orientation is randomly assigned when the skin is opened or dropped and can be identified using a “Pattern ID” which is a number between 1-1000. (You can find Pattern IDs when you inspect the weapon). If two of the same pattern-based skin have the same pattern id, the two skins will be identical. A good analogy is that the Pattern ID acts as coordinates, coordinates that specify how the pattern is placed on the gun, impacting the final look of that particular skin.

_____

Q: Why do Pattern Templates look different/unfamiliar when I compare the image to the weapon skin in the game?

A: Color Images are typically stored in red, green and blue channels that combine additively to represent a large range of color values. For skin patterns, Valve takes advantage of this configuration to store three separate images, creating the final template.

What is a Blue Gem? 

Case hardened Skins are the pinnacle of rare patterns for many collectors. The most basic rule of thumb is that the more blue on the case hardened, the more the skin will cost. The amount of blue can categorize specific patterns into "tiers."
